# NPW planning routines and officer workflow — deep dive

Research for [Deep-analyse NPW planning algorithms and officer workflow](https://github.com/awjreynolds/agentic-satn-compiler/issues/315).

- Retrieved: 2026-08-04
- Scope: executable planning routines, diagnostic algorithms, state/workflow glue, and map interaction in the official NPW/NPT Scotland sources
- Status: research note; **not an accepted SATN ADR and not permission to import NPW runtime or policy**

## Sources and decision summary

Sources are pinned to the commits inspected: [NPW `920584760ccaa03d2adbd5683142383f0825e0f3`](https://github.com/nptscot/npw/tree/920584760ccaa03d2adbd5683142383f0825e0f3), [NPT `86662723f5b1cd26b0dca867ab36960a8c34de79`](https://github.com/nptscot/npt/tree/86662723f5b1cd26b0dca867ab36960a8c34de79), and [first-party `corenet` `aaed15de5c3a4da9ed3b1b5d5220fbd1a373a02f`](https://github.com/nptscot/corenet/tree/aaed15de5c3a4da9ed3b1b5d5220fbd1a373a02f). The [NPW manual](https://github.com/nptscot/npw/blob/920584760ccaa03d2adbd5683142383f0825e0f3/web/src/manual/index.md) is the owning description of the officer workflow.

NPW is an interactive, browser-local editor over a fixed bidirectional road graph. It seeds editable sections, computes diagnostics after edits, and lets an officer trace, inspect, revise, save, and export. NPT/corenet is a batch preprocessor that produces a **reference coherent-network layer**; NPW displays it and the officer traces over it. The two must not be conflated: `corenet` chooses a largest high-demand component and deletes dangles, whereas NPW's runtime never authors new geometry and exposes disconnected/severed results for review.

Adoptable SATN lessons are narrow: demand-seeded finite candidate discovery, explicit high-LoS reachability/severance diagnostics, shortest-path explanation fixtures, exact endpoint snapping, and state/version validation. Reject importing NPW's hidden thresholds, largest-component deletion, dangle deletion, freehand fallback, bidirectionality assumption, browser save schema, PMTiles/UI code, or a composite coherent-network score as canonical SATN policy.

## What should be rejected explicitly

- **No NPW runtime or `corenet` code as canonical SATN planner.** NPW is Apache-2.0, `corenet` MIT, and NPT AGPL-3.0; the licenses are compatible only after a deliberate legal review, and the dependency is not pinned by NPT's installer. NPW's README says public development froze in May 2026 and moved private ([README](https://github.com/nptscot/npw/blob/920584760ccaa03d2adbd5683142383f0825e0f3#L1-L8)).
- **No largest-component or repeated dangle deletion.** These are presentation/cleaning choices that can erase valid rural, island, resilience, or complementary routes.
- **No hidden distribution thresholds, DBSCAN settings, road scores, penalty values, 250/400 m density claims, or LoS table as unversioned SATN policy.** Every adopted profile must be explicit, fingerprinted, and sensitivity-tested.
- **No freehand fallback, automatic `fix_unreachable_poi`, or bidirectional assumption.** A disconnected route is an evidence gap or finite governed candidate, not silently authored geometry.
- **No browser local-storage savefile, PMTiles, Svelte stores, mutation counter, or live downloader as evidence authority.** Use typed replayable state, source hashes, licence/limitation metadata, and atomic publication.
- **No composite “coherent network” score.** Preserve demand, road class, directness, LoS, severance, access, comfort, and uncertainty as separate inspectable evidence families.

## Recommended SATN proving sequence

1. **Evidence adapter:** pinned NPT-like demand and geometry-match fixture; stable IDs, unmatched/conflict states, source/CRS/profile fingerprints.
2. **Graph diagnostics:** two components, one dangling edge, one low-LoS barrier, one explicit crossing; exact reachability/severance witnesses and no geometry mutation.
3. **Finite candidate discovery:** high-demand seeds plus two profile sensitivities; retain all candidates and rejection reasons, with no largest-component pruning.
4. **Officer scenario overlay:** two valid candidate options and one stale option; accept only compiler-authored IDs, preserve divergence and Network Gap states, and reject freehand geometry.
5. **Projection check:** map layers/legends/details agree with canonical GeoJSON/GeoPackage/PDF and remain correct after input-order permutation.

These fixtures should be the smallest proving boundary before any production adapter is considered. They test the useful officer/network-quality behaviors while preventing foreign UI state, hidden policy, or lossy cleaning from becoming SATN authority.
