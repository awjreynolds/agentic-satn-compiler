# Source corridors and visible planning departures

- Status: accepted for the planned TypeSafe rebuild
- Date: 2026-09-19
- Decision: [canonical owner decision](https://github.com/awjreynolds/agentic-satn-compiler/issues/434#issuecomment-5743559472)
- Supersedes: ADR 0026's mandatory selected-baseline alignment membership only for the planned rebuild; the old production/tag remains unchanged

The owner permits justified full or partial exclusions from the proposed network, but every in-scope A-road remains a mandatory Planning Corridor. A Corridor Departure retains the affected source sections, attribution, reason, evidence, any alternative and the lost or unresolved connection; it remains distinct from a gap or an unassessed corridor. Evidence inventory, provenance and explicit unknowns remain required.

On the final default map, departures—particularly A-road departures—remain prominent: show the original affected sections as dashed geometry with a distinct legend and explanatory text, including the decision and reason and any replacement or unresolved connection. This depiction does not add the original geometry to selected connectivity.
