# OSM active-travel infrastructure enters Asset Accounting

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-18

Pinned OpenStreetMap ways whose explicit tags identify active-travel infrastructure or cycle access are admitted automatically as OSM Active Travel Assets. Missing condition, access or continuity evidence may affect intervention classification, candidate participation or create an Evidence Request, but it cannot erase the mapped asset; deterministic compiler decisions remain inspectable and officers may override them through the existing attributable decision ledger. This rejects both NCN-only asset discovery and per-asset human approval because either would silently omit reusable infrastructure from the SATN.
